Flagellar/motility gene locations — chromosome vs. plasmid

6,356 gene records from the combined (string-match + OrthoFinder+eggNOG-verified) dataset, classified by where each gene's contig is identified in its genome assembly's FASTA header ("plasmid pXXX" vs. "complete genome/sequence" vs. no explicit designation on a draft contig).
"Unclassified" = draft (whole-genome-shotgun) contigs with no explicit chromosome/plasmid label in the assembly header — almost certainly chromosomal fragments, just not confidently labelled as such. 9 records (1 genome, GCF_028649595.1) excluded: that genome's GFF references contigs absent from its own FASTA file, a data mismatch between the two source files for that one assembly.
