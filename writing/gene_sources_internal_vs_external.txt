Source data: gene_order/gene_order_combined.json (final merged gene set,
eggNOG-confirmed orthology calls only), curated list from
scripts/03_gene_order/gene_order.py, merge rules from
scripts/06_orthology/build_combined_gene_order.py.
Counts are GENOMES (of 166) carrying the gene, not gene copies.

Definitions
  String match     gene= tag in the GFF matches a curated name (73 names)
  Internal seed    a string-matched protein; its OrthoFinder orthogroup is
                   expanded and eggNOG-confirmed members are added
  External seed    a C. jejuni NCTC 11168 reference protein; its best
                   DIAMOND hit's orthogroup is expanded the same way
